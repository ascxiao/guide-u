Our project uses Provider + ChangeNotifier for MVVM state management, Supabase with row-level security for secure 
persistence, and a policy-driven transactional model where student report submissions are intentionally append-only 
(create/read) to preserve integrity and auditability, while full mutation operations are available in modules where 
update/delete are logically valid.
